University Academic Attendance & Management Guidelines

The Smart College Attendance Portal serves as the central digital platform for academic attendance tracking, course administration, and semester compliance. Designed for students, professors, and administrative coordinators, this system ensures transparent, accurate, and real-time records across all university programs.

Real-Time Student Analytics

Students can securely log in to view updated attendance counts, subject-wise attendance percentages, detailed absence histories, and active alert notifications for subjects requiring attention.

Faculty Digital Roll-Call

Faculty members can efficiently record class attendance, verify daily student status, manage course rosters, and access consolidated semester attendance logs with single-click reporting.

75% Minimum Attendance Standard

Under university academic regulations, all enrolled students are required to maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in theory and lab coursework to be eligible for semester examinations.

Need technical assistance? Students encountering credential issues or discrepancies in course attendance should report directly to their department faculty advisor or the campus registrar office.
